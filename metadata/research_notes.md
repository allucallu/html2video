## 3. Market Research & Search Query Findings

We analyzed search demand, saturation, and buyer intent across four core search clusters in the Adobe Stock Video catalog:

### Query 1: `new year countdown clock celebration loop 4k`
- **Approximate Results**: ~5,200 results (High seasonal demand, peaks Nov–Jan each year, high purchase velocity)
- **Top Competitor Recurring Keywords**: `countdown clock`, `midnight countdown`, `clock striking twelve`, `new year eve`, `party countdown`, `confetti explosion`, `party popper`, `champagne toast`, `celebration loop`
- **Buyer Persona**: Television broadcasters, corporate event organizers, social media video editors creating holiday greetings, party promos, and year-end recaps.
- **Winning Title Pattern**: `[Event/Celebration Subject] + [Specific Motion Element] + [Occasion] + Loop` (e.g., *Midnight Countdown Clock Striking Twelve New Year Party Loop*).

### Query 2: `business goal achievement bullseye target loop`
- **Approximate Results**: ~4,100 results (Perpetual year-round B2B demand)
- **Top Competitor Recurring Keywords**: `bullseye`, `target goal`, `goal hit`, `achievement icon`, `success goal`, `kpi achievement`, `rocket launch`, `trophy cup`, `growth pillars`, `mission accomplished`
- **Buyer Persona**: Corporate marketing agencies, HR onboarding video creators, SaaS pitch deck animators, leadership keynote presentation editors.
- **Winning Title Pattern**: `[Achievement Symbol] + [Action/Progress] + [Milestone Context] + Loop` (e.g., *Bullseye Target Goal Hit and Arrow Center Achievement Loop*).

### Query 3: `topographic map isolines contour loop 4k`
- **Approximate Results**: ~2,300 results (Moderate competition, high artistic and documentary value)
- **Top Competitor Recurring Keywords**: `topographic isolines`, `noise cartography`, `elevation contours`, `bathymetric isolines`, `contour lines`, `swiss editorial`, `scientific cartography`, `terrain elevation`
- **Buyer Persona**: Documentary filmmakers, outdoor lifestyle brand editors, GIS data visualizers, luxury tech commercial editors.
- **Opportunity**: Very few true 4K seamless procedural math loops exist; most competitors offer low-resolution pre-rendered stock with visible compression artifacts.

### Query 4: `orbital resonance planet epicycle astronomy loop`
- **Approximate Results**: ~950 results (Low competition / High-value scientific niche)
- **Top Competitor Recurring Keywords**: `orbital resonance`, `astronomical orrery`, `epicycle orrery`, `sacred geometry`, `celestial mechanics`, `planetary orbit`, `pentagram orbit`, `astronomy simulation`
- **Buyer Persona**: Science communicators, educational documentary editors, museum exhibit designers, ambient music visualizers.
- **Opportunity**: The Earth-Venus 8:13 resonance pentagram is a viral geometry topic on YouTube and TikTok, making it a high-intent educational purchase.
