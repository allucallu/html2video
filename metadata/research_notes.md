## 3. Market Research & Search Query Findings

We examined competitor assets and search term performance in the Adobe Stock Video catalog across four primary market sectors:

### Query 1: `fintech payment animation loop 4k`
- **Approximate Results**: ~4,800 results (Moderate saturation)
- **Top Competitor Recurring Keywords**: `fintech`, `cashless payment`, `contactless`, `pos terminal`, `nfc`, `digital wallet`, `money transfer`, `qr code`, `instant checkout`, `online banking`
- **Buyer Persona**: Corporate video editors creating B2B SaaS promos, banking product explainers, and mobile retail case studies.
- **Winning Title Pattern**: `[Technology Subject] + [Action/Flow] + [Business Context] + Loop` (e.g., *Fintech QR Code Payment Scan and Contactless POS Gateway Loop*).

### Query 2: `cybersecurity firewall network loop`
- **Approximate Results**: ~6,500 results (Medium-High competition, strong sustained demand)
- **Top Competitor Recurring Keywords**: `cybersecurity`, `firewall`, `network security`, `data protection`, `encryption`, `cyber defense`, `server protection`, `infosec`, `digital vault`, `threat defense`
- **Buyer Persona**: Enterprise IT security marketers, tech conference video teams, cybersecurity startup landing pages.
- **Winning Title Pattern**: `[Defense Mechanism] + [Network Target] + [Visual Style] + Loop` (e.g., *Cybersecurity Data Firewall Shield Network Protection Motion Loop*).

### Query 3: `neo brutalism sticker motion graphics`
- **Approximate Results**: ~1,200 results (Low competition / High-growth trending niche)
- **Top Competitor Recurring Keywords**: `neo brutalism`, `sticker`, `bold outline`, `pop art`, `sale badge`, `discount label`, `price tag`, `coin spin`, `creator economy`, `social media sticker`
- **Buyer Persona**: YouTube video editors, TikTok/Reels content creators, motion designers looking for punchy graphic overlays that stand out against bland minimalism.
- **Opportunity**: Very few high-resolution 4K seamless loops exist in this style; most competitors offer static PNGs or non-looping low-res clips.

### Query 4: `dither fluid wave black background loop`
- **Approximate Results**: ~850 results (Sparse / High artist & VJ demand)
- **Top Competitor Recurring Keywords**: `dither`, `1 bit`, `halftone`, `retro fluid`, `topography wave`, `generative art`, `lo-fi`, `monochrome`, `vj loop`, `screen blend`
- **Buyer Persona**: Music video creators, live concert visualizers (VJs), aesthetic techno event promoters, indie game trailers.
- **Opportunity**: Extremely high Screen/Add blend utility with solid black background `#000000`.
