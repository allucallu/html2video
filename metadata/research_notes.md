## 2. Market Research Findings (Adobe Stock Video Tab)

Research was conducted across primary stock video queries matching the visual subjects of the 14 assets.

### Query Analysis Summary

#### Query 1: `"hand drawn animation black background"` / `"doodle overlay loop"`
- **Approximate Results:** ~28,000–35,000 video clips globally.
- **Market Saturation:** Moderate. High demand for clean YouTube explainer video doodle overlays (arrows, checkmarks, circles, underlines) rendered at 4K resolution.
- **Top Recurring Words in Titles:** `Hand drawn`, `doodle`, `scribble`, `arrow`, `circle`, `checkmark`, `line boil`, `black background`, `loop`, `overlay`, `marker`.
- **Top Recurring Keywords:** `hand drawn`, `doodle`, `animation`, `line boil`, `marker`, `sketch`, `explainer video`, `youtube`, `overlay`, `black background`, `seamless loop`, `4k`, `yellow`, `white`, `infographic`.

#### Query 2: `"magic circle loop black background"` / `"arcane runes vfx"`
- **Approximate Results:** ~9,500–14,000 video clips.
- **Market Saturation:** Moderate-to-high demand for fantasy gaming and cinematic anime overlays. Buyers look for complex multi-ring sacred geometry and non-cluttered particles.
- **Top Recurring Words in Titles:** `Magic circle`, `summoning circle`, `arcane`, `runes`, `sacred geometry`, `glowing`, `loop`, `black background`, `vfx`.
- **Top Recurring Keywords:** `magic circle`, `summoning`, `runes`, `alchemy`, `spell casting`, `fantasy`, `game vfx`, `rotating`, `gold`, `lapis blue`, `black background`, `overlay`, `seamless loop`, `4k`.

#### Query 3: `"lightning strike animation loop"` / `"electric vfx black background"`
- **Approximate Results:** ~16,000–22,000 video clips.
- **Market Saturation:** High volume of generic CGI bolts, but **low competition for hand-painted 2D game VFX with multi-branching forks and non-clipping safe margins**.
- **Top Recurring Words in Titles:** `Lightning strike`, `electric bolt`, `thunderbolt`, `energy`, `flash`, `loop`, `black background`, `vfx`.
- **Top Recurring Keywords:** `lightning`, `electric`, `thunderbolt`, `discharge`, `branching`, `plasma`, `energy vfx`, `game vfx`, `ice blue`, `cobalt`, `black background`, `overlay`, `loop`, `4k`.

#### Query 4: `"scrapbook sticker animation"` / `"washi tape loop"`
- **Approximate Results:** ~3,500–5,200 video clips.
- **Market Saturation:** **Low Saturation / High Opportunity.** Vlogging and lifestyle creators search constantly for retro stationery, paper clips, and washi tape stickers for aesthetic video edits and digital journaling.
- **Top Recurring Words in Titles:** `Washi tape`, `paper clip`, `scrapbook`, `sticker`, `stationery`, `fluttering`, `loop`, `black background`.
- **Top Recurring Keywords:** `washi tape`, `paper clip`, `scrapbook`, `sticker`, `paper craft`, `vlog overlay`, `bullet journal`, `retro`, `cute`, `black background`, `loop`, `overlay`, `4k`.
